FILES TO UPLOAD TO CPANEL (for the updated Nord Breeze Media site)
===============================================================

Upload these from your folder "Nord Breeze Digital WEBSITE" into 
public_html (or your web root) on cPanel. Keep the same structure.

REQUIRED – main site:
  index.html
  styles.css
  script.js
  nordbreezemedia.png

REQUIRED – pages (upload to same folder as index.html):
  packages.html
  seo-packages.html
  social-media-services.html
  process.html
  drift-package.html
  current-package.html
  boreal-package.html
  aurora-package.html

OPTIONAL – only if your live site uses them:
  neon-analytics.svg
  neon-designer.svg
  neon-developer.svg
  team-illustration.html

DO NOT UPLOAD (for your use only, not for the live site):
  Start-Server.bat
  HOW-TO-VIEW-LOCALLY.txt
  FILES-TO-UPLOAD.txt (this file)
  remove_background_guide.html
  clean_laptop_icon.html
  remove_bg.py

TIP: In cPanel File Manager, upload the whole folder, then move the 
files from "Nord Breeze Digital WEBSITE" into public_html (or merge 
with existing site files). Make sure nordbreezemedia.png is in the 
same folder as index.html.
